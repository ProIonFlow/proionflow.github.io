The honest starting point

Savings are counterfactual. We can't observe what you'd have paid without PIF — we can only model it.

So we never assert a savings figure as fact. We derive it, show the inputs, and state the assumption — then label how confident that number is.

The four evidence tiers

Every figure in your ledger carries one of these labels, so you always know how solid it is.

What we don't claim

{{ icoInfo }}No per-device attribution from a utility bill — bills don't itemize by device.
{{ icoInfo }}Modeled figures are estimates (≈), not measurements.
{{ icoInfo }}Savings vary by home, devices, and utility rates. No guarantee, and no make-good credit for a modeled shortfall.
{{ icoShield }}The one refundable case is if PIF ends your subscription — then we refund the unused part of your fee. If you cancel, your plan runs through the period you've paid for and isn't refunded (see Terms).

Versioning & changelog

Every savings figure records the methodology version that computed it, and its link resolves to that version — a past figure stays interpretable under the method that produced it. Superseded versions stay permanently reachable.
